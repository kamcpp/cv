QA Engineer (Strong Junior)

We're seeking a passionate and skilled Junior QA Engineer to join our dynamic team in Vienna. As a key member of our engineering organization, you'll play a vital role in building high quality cutting-edge web applications that delight our users.

What You'll Do

  • Perform functional testing of web applications
  • Participate in all SCRUM meetings
  • Validate API requests, responses, and business rules
  • Create, execute, and maintain test cases, checklists, and regression test suites
  • Collaborate closely with developers and product managers to identify missing or unclear acceptance criteria and ensure feature quality
  • Report defects with detailed reproduction steps and supporting evidence
  • Verify application behavior using logs, database queries, browser developer tools, and API responses
  • Participate in smoke, regression, cross-browser, exploratory, and release validation testing
  • Contribute to continuous improvement of QA processes and test documentation

What You'll Bring

  • Good understanding of software testing principles and SDLC/STLC
  • Knowledge of functional, regression, smoke, sanity, cross-browser, and accessibility testing
  • Ability to design effective positive, negative, and edge-case test scenarios
  • Experience writing clear and structured bug reports
  • Familiarity with test management and defect tracking tools (TestRail, Jira, or similar).
  • Understanding of test documentation, test cases, and checklists
  • Strong analytical and logical thinking
  • Excellent attention to detail
  • Curiosity and willingness to investigate issues thoroughly
  • Ability to manage priorities and work independently after onboarding
  • Fast learner with a proactive attitude
  • Understanding of REST APIs and HTTP protocol (Postman or similar API tools)
  • Basic understanding of authentication mechanisms (API Key, Basic Auth, Bearer Token)
  • Ability to investigate defects using application logs and API responses
  • Ability to isolate issues and collect useful information for developers
  • Curiosity and willingness to dig deeper into the root cause of problems
  • Good written and spoken English. German is not required but appreciated
  • 1+ years of work experience as a QA Engineer

Why Join Us

Competitive compensation and benefits package, including:

  • Competitive salary based on your experience and skills
  • High-quality IT equipment, i.e. modern Apple laptop, gaming chair, vertically adjustable desk
  • Free Wien 365 travel pass
  • Possibility to work from any EU country, up to 30 days / year

Meaningful work on impactful projects that make a real difference.

A collaborative and supportive team environment where your ideas are valued and respected.

Opportunities for professional growth and development, including access to training, and mentorship..

A culture of innovation that encourages experimentation, creativity, and continuous learning.

E X C E L L E N T C O F F E E

Work Environment:

  • Hybrid work model: We embrace a hybrid work approach with a combination of in-office presence and remote work.
  • In-office presence is required for a minimum of three days per week. This fosters strong team collaboration and in-person interaction.

Ready to make your mark?

If you're a talented and passionate Backend Engineer who is eager to join a dynamic and growing company and share our commitment to writing high-quality, well-tested code and thrive in a collaborative in-office environment, we encourage you to apply!

We offer a full-time position with a collective agreement minimum salary of EUR 33.406,8 gross/year. This will be overpaid depending on your professional experience and qualifications.
